# Policy implementation claims

[POLICY-PROGRAM.md](../POLICY-PROGRAM.md) is the single current model and learning
program. Its authority comes from the operator requirements preserved in
[SPECIFICATION.md](../SPECIFICATION.md), including the common final representation
for policy and value outputs.

[FEATURE-GRAM-PROVENANCE.md](../FEATURE-GRAM-PROVENANCE.md) and
[POLICY-HEAD-AUDIT.md](../POLICY-HEAD-AUDIT.md) document withdrawn interpretations.
Older implementation manifests and assertions in removed tests do not establish
additional requirements.

Historical source, numerical and match records remain in
[the September 6 evidence directory](../../measurements/policy-feature-integration-20260906/).
They describe their recorded implementations and do not establish learned playing
strength, arbitrary expressivity, persistent allocation or runtime behavior for a
later model. Current source changes require their own accurately scoped account.
