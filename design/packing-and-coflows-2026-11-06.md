  - Claude, #2487, 17:30:09Z: «The compiler should bin-pack. A program that writes h every tick declares a value, not an address. Assigning that value's pages across repeated invocations, so no two simultaneously live iterations share a byte, is page assignment (eager-execution.md, Freeing 7).»

### 1.2 The algorithm choice of 11-02, with its citations (the closest thing to "the assignment")

- nov03.txt #2682, T=2026-11-02T23:01:58Z (15:01): “now what does the literature on memory allocation and communications
  and dependency resolution in functional programming say about our scheme here? is the solver we want here something
  we can copy in whole or in composed parts entirely from existing solvers?”
- Claude, nov03.txt #2683, 23:04:34Z. Its citations, as written («from memory, with no live search this turn» is its own caveat):
  - «DSA is NP-hard (Garey and Johnson, SR2). Approximation algorithms exist: Gergov's 3-approximation (SODA 1999) and Buchsbaum et al.'s 2+ε (STOC 2003).»
  - «Greedy by size: place the largest first, at the lowest offset with no conflict among items already placed. This is TFLite's planner (Pisarchyk and Lee 2020, near the lower bound on DNNs) and XLA's GlobalDecreasingSizeBestFitHeap. It works on any conflict relation, not only intervals.»
  - «Exact or branch-and-bound: MiniMalloc (Moffitt, Google, ASPLOS 2023, open source) and TelaMalloc (Maas et al., ASPLOS 2023, heuristics plus constraint programming). Both assume lifetimes are intervals in one total order.»
  - «Desnos, Pelcat, Nezan and Aridhi (PREESM, 2013–2016) build a memory exclusion graph from a DAG's precedence.» and «Strout et al.'s schedule-independent storage mapping (ASPLOS 1998)».
  - Joint order and placement as ILPs: «OLLA (Steiner et al. 2022) and MODeL (ICML 2023)», judged «too slow for the 8 s bound».
  - Its stated plan: «4. Placement: greedy by size over that relation, with pins placed first in fixed bins. This replaces the first-fit-by-begin I'd written».
- What was decided, as recorded in design/page-maps-2026-11-02.md item 10 (mesh c77d4217, 11-02 15:25; file later deleted, see §4):
  «Placement, first pass, over the exclusion relation: pins first; then the items a contiguous-typed function touches, greedy by size, each one range at the lowest position of the first bin where every run it would meet is ordered with it; then every other item, page by page, from the lowest free position, in a topological order of first accesses. Page by page leaves no external fragmentation, so the hardness of dynamic storage allocation touches the contiguous items alone: the reason pages exist (Denning 1970).»
  - Also: «Exact solvers (MiniMalloc, TelaMalloc) and the integer programs of OLLA and MODeL bound how far a pass is from optimal. [...] so they are references, not the compile path.»
