# Policy execution and learning data

[POLICY-PROGRAM.md](POLICY-PROGRAM.md) is the current complete policy flow: native
inputs, row projections and mixing, common final policy/value representation,
sampling, private-view integration, applied-source attribution and optimization.
It replaces the separate model and loss manifests formerly maintained here.

Map and cart-path construction are described in
[CARTPATHS.md](../xonotic/payload/CARTPATHS.md). The literal scoring and reward
object is defined in [CART-GAME-CONTRACT.md](CART-GAME-CONTRACT.md).
[TRAINING-CONTINUATION.md](TRAINING-CONTINUATION.md) describes durable source
retention, optimizer recovery and engine outcome delivery.

Earlier numerical and native-run records remain historical evidence for their
recorded source. Their test suites and reproduction harnesses were removed at
the operator's instruction; they are not requirements or completion criteria.
